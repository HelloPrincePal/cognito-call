## The Problem

Every meeting holds useful information — decisions, action items, who-said-what — but capturing it is a hassle, and every existing option asks you to give something up:

- **Cloud note-takers** (Otter, Fireflies, bots that join your call) stream your private conversations to someone else's servers, often behind a **subscription** or **per-minute** pricing, and require accounts and consent gymnastics.
- **Manual note-taking** pulls your attention out of the conversation, and you still miss things.
- **DIY transcription** means juggling ffmpeg, Whisper, diarization models, and glue code yourself.

For anything confidential — client calls, interviews, internal strategy — **sending your audio to a third party is a non-starter.**

## The Solution

Cognito Call does all of it **locally**:

- 🎙️ **Captures both sides** of the call — your microphone *and* the remote participants — as separate, clean audio streams, plus the full screen video.
- 🔒 **100% offline & private** — transcription, speaker separation, and summarization all run on your Mac via Apple's **MLX**. No internet round-trip, no accounts, no tokens, no recurring cost.
- 🧠 **Turns recordings into intelligence** — a speaker-labeled, sentence-level transcript, an executive summary, and action items, generated by a local LLM.
- ⚡ **Works with your meetings** — Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, or any tab with audio. On Google Meet it can even reuse the live captions for instant results.
- 🎬 **Karaoke playback** — replay the recording with each word highlighted in sync, and click any word to jump to that moment.

You own the recordings, the transcripts, and the models — everything sits in plain folders on your disk.

## Installation

Cognito Call is **two pieces that work together**:

1. A **Chrome Extension** — records the meeting into a folder in `~/Downloads/CognitoCall/`.
2. A **macOS Desktop App** — reads that folder and produces the transcript + AI notes.

Install both. Requirements: **macOS on Apple Silicon** (M-series) and **Google Chrome**. The app installer handles everything else (Python environment, models, and `ffmpeg`).

### Step 1 — Chrome Extension

1. Download the latest **[cognito-call-extension.zip](https://github.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/releases/download/v0.7.1/cognito-call-extension.zip)**. *(macOS Safari auto-expands `.zip` files into a folder on download.)*
2. If it's still a `.zip`, double-click to extract it.
3. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
5. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select the unzipped `packages/extension` folder.
6. Click the Cognito Call icon in the toolbar and **grant microphone permission** when prompted.

> 📖 Want to know exactly what it captures and how? See **[How the Extension Works](#how-the-extension-works)**.

### Step 2 — Desktop App

Run **one** of the commands below in Terminal to install the app to `/Applications/Cognito Call.app`. Pick the **model tier** that matches your Mac:

**⚡ Lite — for MacBook Air / 8–16 GB RAM (default, fast)**
Lightweight models (`whisper-base` + `gemma-2b`). Fast and comfortable on an Air; good-enough quality for most calls.
```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/install.sh | bash
```

**💎 Pro — for 24 GB+ Macs / MacBook Pro (best quality)**
The best models that run comfortably on 24 GB (`whisper-large-v3-turbo` + `Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-4bit`). Much more accurate transcripts and reliable AI notes — at the cost of more RAM, a slower run, and a ~10 GB model download on first use.
```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/install.sh | bash -s -- --pro
```

> **Prefer to build from source?**
> ```bash
> git clone https://github.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call.git
> cd cognito-call
> ./build-local.sh          # add --pro for the larger models
> ```

> **macOS Gatekeeper:** on first launch, if macOS shows a security warning, right-click `Cognito Call.app` in Finder and choose **Open**.

> 📖 Curious about the offline AI pipeline? See **[How the App Works](#how-the-app-works)**. You can switch tiers or uninstall anytime — see **[Configuration & Management](#configuration--management)**.

## How the Extension Works

The extension is the **recorder**. It's a Manifest V3 Chrome extension that leans on an **Offscreen Document** to keep recording reliably even when Chrome throttles background pages.

**What it captures.** When you hit record on a meeting tab, it grabs two independent audio sources and the video, and saves **three separate files** so the app can process each cleanly:

| File | Contents |
| :--- | :--- |
| `video.webm` | Full screen/tab video with mixed audio |
| `tab.opus` | Remote participants' audio only (via `tabCapture`) |
| `mic.opus` | Your microphone only (via `getUserMedia`) |

Keeping your mic and the remote audio **separate** is what lets the app label "you" vs. the other speakers accurately.

**How it saves.** Each session becomes a timestamped folder in `~/Downloads/CognitoCall/` (e.g. `2026-08-01_14-30-00/`). To move gigabytes of video without hitting Chrome's ~64 MB messaging limit, the offscreen document hands the service worker a lightweight `blob:` URL and lets `chrome.downloads` pull the data straight from memory.

**Seekable files.** Chrome's `MediaRecorder` writes live streams with no duration header or keyframe index, so players can't scrub them. A built-in pure-JS remuxer (`webm-fixer.js`) rewrites each file on stop to inject a proper `Duration` and a `Cues` seek index — so your recordings scrub correctly in QuickTime, VLC, and Chrome.

**Google Meet fast-path.** On `meet.google.com`, a content script watches the live-caption panel and saves the caption text + speaker names to `captions.json`. When present, the app uses these directly and **skips the heavy Whisper pass on the remote audio** — near-instant transcripts.

**Safe by default.** You keep hearing the meeting normally (the captured tab audio is piped to your speakers), a red **REC** badge sits on the toolbar the whole time, and recordings **auto-stop and save** if you close the meeting tab or hit a **3-hour cap** — so a forgotten recording can't run forever.

## How the App Works

The desktop app is the **brain**. It's a native Tauri (Rust) + React app that plays your recordings and runs the offline AI pipeline. When you open it, it shows a gallery of your sessions and **automatically starts transcribing the newest one** that doesn't have notes yet (press **Esc** anytime to cancel).

Under the hood it launches a Python sidecar (shown as `cognito-assistant` in Activity Monitor) that runs, in order:

1. **Transcription** — `mlx-whisper` transcribes `mic.opus` and `tab.opus` separately with millisecond word timestamps. Calls longer than 30 minutes are sliced into 15-minute windows so memory stays flat (~constant RAM) even on 3-hour recordings.
2. **Speaker diarization** — `simple-diarizer` runs spectral clustering on the remote audio to split it into `Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`, … (skipped entirely when Google Meet captions are available).
3. **Stitching** — your mic track (labeled with your name) is merged with the diarized remote speakers and sorted chronologically into a single timeline. Silence-hallucination artifacts are filtered out here.
4. **AI notes** — a local LLM runs a map-reduce pass over the transcript to clean fragments into full sentences, attribute speakers from context, and produce an **executive summary** and **action items**.

The results are written back into the same session folder:

```
~/Downloads/CognitoCall/2026-08-01_14-30-00/
  ├── video.webm         # screen/tab video
  ├── mic.opus           # your microphone audio
  ├── tab.opus           # remote participants audio
  ├── transcript.json    # speaker-labeled, sentence-level transcript
  ├── metadata.json      # inferred meeting title
  └── summary.json       # executive summary + action items
```

Back in the app, the **Karaoke player** highlights each word as the video plays and lets you click any word to jump to that timestamp; the transcript and notes are exportable as JSON or plain text.

> ⚠️ **Don't rename or move the session folders** inside `~/Downloads/CognitoCall/`. The app relies on this exact layout for playback sync and word-level alignment.

## Configuration & Management

Everything is driven by a few one-line commands. Your recordings in `~/Downloads/CognitoCall/` are never touched by any of them.

### Choose or switch model quality (tiers)

Re-run the installer with or without `--pro` at any time — it rewrites `~/.cognitocall/models.json` and, to save space, **removes the other tier's model weights** so you never store both at once.

```bash
# Lite  (whisper-base + gemma-2b)            — fast, Air-friendly
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/install.sh | bash

# Pro   (whisper-large-v3-turbo + Qwen2.5-14B) — best quality, 24 GB+
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/install.sh | bash -s -- --pro
```

### Update to the latest version

```bash
# Desktop app (keeps all recordings & models):
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/install.sh | bash

# If you built from source:
git pull && ./build-local.sh
```

**Extension:** re-download the [latest zip](https://github.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/releases/download/v0.7.1/cognito-call-extension.zip), extract it over your existing folder, then click 🔄 **Reload** on the card at `chrome://extensions`.

### Uninstall / free up disk

```bash
# Remove everything (app + models + Python env + settings):
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/uninstall.sh | bash -s -- --all

# Remove only the app, keep the models:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/uninstall.sh | bash -s -- --app-only

# Remove only the downloaded AI models (keep the app):
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelloPrincePal/cognito-call/main/uninstall.sh | bash -s -- --models-only
```

### Where things are stored

| What | Location | Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Your recordings & transcripts | `~/Downloads/CognitoCall/` | grows with use |
| App environment (Python venv, ffmpeg, config) | `~/.cognitocall/` | ~small |
| AI model weights | `~/.cache/huggingface/hub/` | Lite ≈ 1.5 GB · Pro ≈ 10 GB (active tier only) |

Models download **on first use**, not during install, and only your active tier is kept on disk.

### First launch & personalization

On first open, the app asks for your name (e.g. **Ram**). It's used to label your microphone track and is passed to the local LLM as context so it can accurately attribute questions, answers, and turns during summarization.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Desktop UI:** Tauri v2 (Rust) + React 18 + TypeScript
- **Recorder:** Chrome MV3 extension — MediaRecorder API + Offscreen Document
- **Speech-to-Text:** `mlx-whisper` — 4-bit Whisper on Apple Silicon (`base` → `large-v3-turbo` by tier)
- **Diarization:** `simple-diarizer` — spectral clustering over PyTorch speaker embeddings
- **Local LLM:** `mlx-lm` — map-reduce pipeline (`gemma-2-2b` → `Qwen2.5-14B` by tier)
- **Fast-path captions:** Google Meet live-caption extraction via `MutationObserver`
